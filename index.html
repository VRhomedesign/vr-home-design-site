About us

We take a home from a first sketch to a finished build — and let you walk through it before a single stake goes in the ground.

VR Home Design & Drafting is a Texas-based visualization and drafting studio providing builders, developers, contractors, and homeowners with everything needed to plan, design, and bring a project to life. From photorealistic 3D renderings to accurate lumber takeoffs and site plans, we blend creativity with construction expertise to deliver clear, detailed, and stunning results.

Rooted in Bryan/College Station with a strong background in homebuilding and interior design, we understand the real-world demands of today’s market — whether you’re presenting a spec home or planning a remodel.
