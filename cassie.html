About this plan

Description

The Cassie is a two-story farmhouse with four bedrooms and three and a half baths, designed around a central great room, dining, and kitchen space that opens straight off the front foyer. The foyer rises to a two-story open entry, and the great room is anchored by a fireplace with built-ins on either side. The kitchen features a large island with sink and dishwasher, plenty of counter space, and a walk-in pantry tucked just off the cooking area.

The primary suite sits on its own wing of the main floor, with a spacious bedroom, a primary bath with double vanities, a freestanding soaking tub, a separate shower, and a private water closet, plus a large walk-in closet. Between the kitchen and the primary suite, a generous utility room includes a mud bench, laundry, a utility sink, and a half bath.

On the opposite side of the home, two secondary bedrooms share a full bath with separate vanity areas, and a storage room sits off the hallway by the stairs. Upstairs, a cathedral-ceiling loft overlooks the entry and offers flexible space for a game room, media room, or study, alongside a fourth bedroom with its own full bath featuring a soaking tub and separate shower.

Covered porches wrap the front, back, and side of the home for plenty of outdoor living. The exterior pairs white board-and-batten siding with a stone chimney and stone front gable, a black standing-seam metal roof, and stained timber posts on stone piers for a classic modern farmhouse look.
